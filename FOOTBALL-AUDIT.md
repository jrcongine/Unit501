# Football model audit — October 10, 2026

## Implemented

- **Scoring separation:** v2 adds shrunk offense and opposing defense deviations around one league baseline. The previous formula averaged them again after four-game shrinkage, halving their signals. A four-game fixture with 8/40-point offenses and 40/8-point defenses now predicts 8–40 instead of 16–32. This proves the arithmetic change, not real-world accuracy.
- **League environment:** the baseline averages game-weighted points scored and allowed across the verified pool. This matters for FBS schedules with opponents outside that pool. Both scoring metrics must have complete coverage.
- **Low-score simulations:** locate the underlying normal so its nonnegative continuous mean matches the projection. Rounding remains; a zero-point mean stays zero. This avoids extra inflation from simply flooring negative normal draws.
- **Historical replay:** schedule-only replay reconstructs each earlier cutoff, excludes simultaneous/future results from training, reports score/margin/total errors and total bias, and compares the exact previous scoring formula on the same eligible games. The previous formula is accessible only as the fourth `project` argument for audit replay; the app uses v2.
- **Variance and reliability:** after 30 scored earlier predictions, residual RMS and correlation inform later simulations. Ten away-win frequency bins and later-game Brier score expose reliability. This remains a diagnostic, not proof that percentages are accurate. A later extension adds symmetric probability calibration with distinct-game minimums, chronological later-game checks and a lower-Brier activation gate; see FOOTBALL-MODEL.md. No historical bookmaker lines are available, so spread/total profit or prop hit probabilities are not backtested.
- **Loading:** publish complete scoring ranks and historical checks before waiting for national yardage boxes. The UI explicitly says scoring is ready during the build, reports a two-minute stall, times out individual fetches, and retains already-loaded scoring if a later poll fails.
- **Props:** rushing attempts are parsed and projected, with `player_rush_attempts` requested from the FanDuel feed. RB receiving yards and receptions already existed and remain available to any player with recorded receiving stats. Official market keys: https://the-odds-api.com/sports-odds-data/betting-markets.html . Missing quotes remain manual; a supported key does not guarantee FanDuel offers the market for a particular game.
- **Projection audit:** retain the completed team-game calendar as missing placeholders, expose stat coverage, and withhold markets when the latest game is missing and fewer than half of team games have recorded stats. This is a completeness gate, not an inferred participation probability.  when paired usage exists, yardage uses blended season/recent workload multiplied by a blend of pooled and median efficiency. This reduces the influence of a single long-catch game. Missing games remain missing, and recorded negative yardage is retained. Workload changes remain visible.
- **Opponent context:** compare the opponent's complete team defensive metric with that same metric's league average, rather than the player's own team offense. Adjustment remains bounded at ±7.5% and experimental. Yardage ranks arriving after scoring refresh the projection context.
- **Live provider names:** decode known encoded apostrophes for FBS membership and odds matching, including the observed `Hawai&#x27;i` name. Unresolved schools remain unmatched.
- **Team changes and reports:** histories are keyed by team plus player ID. Current fresh, nonempty roster absence withholds a prop as team-unconfirmed without declaring an injury or inferring a trade. Existing unavailable-player gates remain. Old saved prop-line keys still use the original player ID and preserve browser entries.
- **Data reuse:** server requests for player/team boxes share a five-minute in-memory cache across features/visitors. Schedule responses cache one minute. Errors, empty reports and pagination are not cached. Completed ranking boxes retain their existing 24-hour cache. Player history rejects incomplete pagination and missing team boxes, and uses matching season/league regular-season NFL games only.

## Validation and remaining work

`npm test` covers scoring separation, low-score means, chronology, variance fitting, old/new replay, early scoring readiness, usage/efficiency, rush-attempt/RB receiving parsing, team changes, report gates, cache behavior and actual client-script flows including saved lines. The DOM harness tests interactions, not visual rendering. Chromium was unavailable and its download failed; no browser-rendering claim is made.

There are no production API-Sports or Odds API credentials in this workspace. Using the supplied production URL, health and current game data were verified, and the Georgia–Alabama FanDuel spread/total feed returned fresh lines. PR #14 deployed scoring v2 successfully; Railway reported success and the live model asset and health endpoint were checked. Five pregame San José State player reports were checked: Ivey has receiving records of 34 and 150 yards in only two of five completed team games, with no receiving record in the latest game. Missing records are not treated as zero. The updated model withholds this sparse/latest-missing estimate. Production backtest accuracy and the new rushing-attempt feed remain unverified until deployment. No real-data accuracy improvement or betting edge is claimed from fixtures.

Existing schedule-strength, home-field, injury-scenario and wind tests remain in place. Passing/rushing/scoring offense and defense rankings already exist; national yardage ranks still require the full box-score build. Pace/possessions are not explicitly modeled. Historical injury/weather scenarios are absent from replay. Non-FBS schedule strength is still a bounded cross-division assumption.

Production ratings now cover NFL and verified FBS rosters and update with season/recent results. Confirmed same-position replacement scenarios transfer recorded workload, update supported props, and change team scores when complete offensive yardage exists. They compare workload and efficiency rather than multiplying production percentiles into baseline scores. The scenario score conversion is an explicitly unvalidated assumption. Full talent grades and automatic valuations for every position remain unfinished: blocking, detailed coverage, verified starters, replacement quality and complete transaction history are not supplied by the existing box-score model. Multiplying team scores or props by production percentiles would count the same performance twice; this audit does not introduce that shortcut. Prop baselines already change with recorded usage and recent efficiency. Basketball and baseball remain deferred until football's live validation is satisfactory.

The first football fix set was merged as PR #14. The subsequent player/replacement/calibration extension is described in FOOTBALL-MODEL.md. Deployment status must be checked separately from creating a PR.
