# Football injury roles and baseline overlap

NFL and college football use shared automatic injury scoring. College depth discovery reads Ourlads' published directory, resolves a unique FBS school, verifies the page's team/season and publication age (seven days maximum), and matches unique provider roster identities and compatible positions. This is an independent published depth chart, not a confirmed lineup. No matchup-specific player overrides were added. Missing/ambiguous charts leave roles unknown.

A listed first-string role can qualify independently of the recent tackle/reception screen. Second-string players with recorded contributions in at least two recent games use half the existing positional sensitivity; the existing smaller NFL RB2 assumption is retained. Depth rank alone is not proof of playing time. Next listed replacements and their reported statuses are shown separately; neither replacement quality nor exact snaps is inferred.

For current reports, season participation history is matched to the exact IDs and kickoff times in the scoring context. Positive recorded events establish an appearance. Explicit reconciled DNP or an exact historical conference Out report establishes an absence. Missing records, zero statistics, omitted names, and uncertain statuses do not establish absence. Conflicting evidence remains unknown for scoring. Reports from another team/game/season cannot establish an absence.

The extra positional sensitivity is multiplied by played games / baseline games only when participation for the whole baseline is verified. This is an approximate exposure correction to the existing hand-set assumptions, not a calibrated causal model; appearances do not establish full-game snaps, and opponent/recency weighting may differ. All baseline games absent means no additional penalty. Unknown overlap withholds the extra penalty. Current pipeline responses require this check even when history retrieval fails. Legacy responses without the requirement flag retain their explicitly unverified behavior until refreshed. Missing provider identities cannot bypass the overlap check.

The scoring engine and simulator use the same resulting adjustment. NBA/CBB retain their separate shared historical absence-pattern model; football positional assumptions are not copied into basketball.

Source limitation verified October 31: the live Big 12 public endpoint contained this week's seven matchups only. It cannot establish earlier absences for players such as Cole Rusk. The current published reserve listing is not proof he never played or had no prior value. Complete historical participation or explicit earlier reports are still needed. No unsupported point value was invented for this gap.

Validation covers published starter/backup/reserve distinctions, identity/season/source-expiry rejection, low latest tackle starter recognition, reduced contributor sensitivity, partial/all-baseline absences, incomplete/conflicting histories, exact baseline matching, and score-to-simulation propagation. Live directory and Arizona chart parsing were checked; synthetic tests do not prove predictive accuracy or authenticated production injury responses.
